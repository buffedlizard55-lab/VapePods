    What California adds to a pre-tax price

    • 15% cannabis excise tax — charged on gross receipts and included in the amount subject to sales tax. Official: CDTFA — Tax Facts for Cannabis Businesses.
    • 8.625% SF sales & use tax — 7.25% statewide base + 1.375% district. Official rate tables and address lookup: CDTFA city & county rates / rate by address.
    • Local cannabis business tax, if passed on — CDTFA notes a local cannabis business tax passed on to the customer becomes part of gross receipts, so it also attracts excise and sales tax. SF's rate is set by the City; any line on the receipt is the authoritative number. Official: CDTFA tax facts #note6.

    Method — how every line was verified

    1. Official sources only. Prices are read from the retailer's own domain or from the brand's own ordering domain (e.g. STIIIZY's pick-up menu on stiiizy.dispensary.shop). One weekly deal comes from STIIIZY Parkmerced's brand-operated store listing and is labelled as such.
    2. Each row is dated. live means the page was fetched and read on . read on a related page means the price was read on a different page of the same official site (stated in the row's note). not re-checked today means it was read on and left untouched — never silently re-dated.
    3. Tax is never assumed. If a store prints no tax note, the row says “tax not stated” and the site treats it as pre-tax in the estimate.
    4. No third-party prices. Aggregator and lookalike shops were excluded; the single brand-operated store listing used for the Sunday/Wednesday pod deal is flagged in the flags section.
    5. Distances are straight-line from 2161 Irving St (94122) using the addresses on file — not driving distance. Not a substitute for a map.
    6. 21+ / 18+ with medical. Prices change daily: re-open the linked official page before you buy. This page is a snapshot, not a live feed.
