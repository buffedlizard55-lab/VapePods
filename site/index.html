What CA adds on top of a pre-tax price

  • 15% — CA state cannabis excise tax (CDTFA, official; rate restored to 15% eff. Oct 1 2025, next change delayed to FY2028‑29) · cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/cannabis/tax-facts.htm
  • 8.625% — SF combined sales & use tax (7.25% state base + 1.375% city/district) · official reference: CDTFA quarterly rate files · cdtfa.ca.gov
  • SF local cannabis business tax — reported 2.5–5% of gross receipts depending on retailer size; confirm the exact split on your receipt · CBS 8 / Times of San Diego

Rule of thumb for a pre-tax price in SF: expect roughly 25–27% total (excise + sales + local cannabis tax, compounding). Medical (MMIC) purchases may be exempt from state excise/sales tax.

How this was verified (methodology)

  1. Official sources only. Every price was read from the retailer's own website (official domain linked per entry) or from the search-engine index of that official page (marked "search-index" and flagged).
  2. Live fetch vs. index. "live" = the official page was fetched and the price read directly during verification. "index" = the official page is JavaScript-rendered or geo-restricted; the price came from the official site's indexed content and needs a manual click-through.
  3. No third-party prices. Aggregators, deal blogs and unofficial "Stiiizy pod" shops were excluded (see flags). Distances are straight-line estimates computed from the listed addresses — not driving distances.
  4. Tax status per store. Taken from the store's own printed statement where present; otherwise explicitly marked "not stated" — never assumed.
  5. 21+ required. Cannabis products. Prices change constantly — re-verify on the linked official page before you go. This page is a snapshot from .
